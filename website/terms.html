Terms of Service

Draft service information — review required

These sections outline planned topics for Rider and Partner terms. They are not a binding final agreement and must be reviewed before public launch.

Service availability

RideGo is in development and testing. Ride requests, pricing, supported vehicle categories, service areas and availability may change. This preview website does not accept bookings, applications or payments.

Rider responsibilities

Riders should provide accurate pickup and destination details, protect their account credentials, use the service lawfully and follow reasonable trip safety guidance. Cancellation, refunds, disputes and support procedures require approved terms before release.

Partner responsibilities

Partners should maintain valid licences, vehicle registration and other permissions required by applicable law, provide accurate information and follow traffic and safety requirements. Entering a licence number, expiry date or RC image does not mean RideGo has verified or approved a Partner.

Proposed government document verification

RideGo intends to offer a consent-based DigiLocker process for checking supported government-issued RC and Driving Licence records. This integration is not currently available. If introduced, Partners would be asked to authorize access to relevant documents through the official service. Verification results would support, but not replace, checks for applicable vehicle classes, permits, insurance and other eligibility requirements. No successful document retrieval by itself guarantees acceptance as a Partner.

Payments and earnings

The current app testing includes cash-trip accounting and a displayed RideGo commission. Settlement, payouts, final fee terms, tax treatment and dispute handling are not yet established for public use. No guaranteed income or payout timing is promised.

Accounts and enforcement

Account eligibility, suspension, appeals, complaint resolution, liability and governing-law provisions must be reviewed and approved before public release.

Contact and final approval

The final terms must identify the responsible legal entity, effective date, jurisdiction, customer support contact and approved dispute-resolution process.

Important

Bookings, Partner applications and payments are not offered through this preview website.

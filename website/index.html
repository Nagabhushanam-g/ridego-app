Partner onboarding • In preparation

Government document checks, with your consent.

RideGo plans to use an authorized DigiLocker connection to help verify government-issued Registration Certificates (RC) and Driving Licences for Partners.

01

Choose a document

Partners will select RC or Driving Licence verification in the Partner app once the official integration is available.

02

Authorize sharing

Partners will be directed to an official consent process to share supported issued-document information securely.

03

Review eligibility

RideGo will review document validity and relevant vehicle and licence requirements before any Partner approval.

Planned feature only. RideGo does not currently offer DigiLocker login, government verification or automatic Partner approval.

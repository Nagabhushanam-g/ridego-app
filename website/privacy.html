Privacy Policy

Draft privacy information — review required

This page describes the types of information RideGo may process during app testing. It is not the final privacy notice, and it must be reviewed and completed before a public launch.

Information involved in the apps

Depending on the app and feature, RideGo may process account identifiers and contact details, pickup and destination locations, trip records, payment status, device notification tokens, Partner vehicle information, driving licence number and expiry date, and RC images. Information may be processed to operate accounts, match trips, display ride status, keep payment and earnings records, and support Partner verification workflows under development.

Location and permissions

Location information is used for ride-related functionality where the app and device permissions allow it. The precise collection behavior, retention periods, and background-location practices must be confirmed from the released app builds before this notice is finalized.

Document access

Partner RC images are intended to be accessible only to the authenticated submitting Partner under the current Storage rules. Authorized staff review access is not yet implemented. A submitted licence number or RC image is not automatically verified.

Service providers and security

RideGo's development stack uses Firebase services for features such as authentication, database records and file storage. Before release, RideGo must document relevant service providers, data locations, access controls, deletion requests and incident handling.

Your choices and requests

App permissions can be managed in device settings. Official privacy contact details, correction and deletion request procedures, and applicable retention periods will be provided before public launch.

Important

Do not submit personal, identity, location, or payment information through this preview website.

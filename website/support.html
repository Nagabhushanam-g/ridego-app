RideGo Support

Support channels are being prepared

The RideGo apps are in testing. Official customer support contact details will be published before public launch. This website currently has no monitored support inbox or live chat.

Common questions

Can I book a ride on this website?

No. The website is a preview and cannot accept ride bookings.

Where can I download the apps?

Public downloads are not available yet. Only designated testers should use development APKs.

Can I apply to become a Partner here?

No. Public Partner registration is not available through this website.

Does RideGo verify RC and Driving Licence documents through DigiLocker?

Not yet. RideGo is preparing an authorized, consent-based DigiLocker integration for the Partner app. The verification option is not active and no government document is currently verified through DigiLocker by RideGo.

Will uploading an RC image automatically approve me?

No. An RC image or entered licence number is only a temporary submission. Government document checks and a separate eligibility review are required before any approval process can be introduced.

Can I submit identity documents here?

No. Do not send licence numbers, registration documents, payment details or other personal information through this preview.

Important

For safety emergencies, contact local emergency services. This preview page is not monitored for urgent assistance.

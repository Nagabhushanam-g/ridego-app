Safety at RideGo

Safety guidance for our testing phase

RideGo is testing trip workflows and preparing formal safety processes for Riders and Partners. The guidance below is general advice, not a claim that safety features or emergency assistance are operational.

Before a trip

Confirm the pickup and destination shown in the app. Check that the vehicle and Partner details match the trip information available to you. Do not begin a ride if something seems wrong.

During a trip

Use the trip information shown in the app and stay aware of your surroundings. Never share passwords, login codes or sensitive account details with another person.

For Partners

Carry a valid driving licence, follow local traffic rules, and ensure the vehicle is legally permitted and safe to operate. Submitting details in RideGo does not constitute approval or regulatory verification.

Important

For an immediate emergency, contact your local emergency services. RideGo's emergency-support processes are not yet publicly available.
